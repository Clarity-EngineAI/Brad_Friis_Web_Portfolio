# Read me first

Two files in this folder:

- **`READ-ME-FIRST.md`** (this one). Plain English,, about ten minutes. Written so you can rule on
  the one open decision without reading the plan.
- **`PLAN-fit-diagnostic.md`**. The full approved build plan, 600 lines. Engineering detail:
  data shapes, accessibility, print layout, build sequence. You do not need it to decide.

Nothing has been built. No code exists. The build is held until you sign off the eight numbers in
section 3 below.

---

## 1. What the thing is

A new page on your site at `/fit/`.

A hiring manager or recruiter lands on it and answers **eight multiple-choice questions about
their own business**, not about you. Where is revenue leaking. What happens to an account's spend
after two years. How much of what they sell actually gets used. How this role's number is set.

Two minutes later they get a report that scores **how well your evidenced record overlaps the
situation they just described**, including, explicitly, where it does not. Plus a section saying
what you would need from them in the first ninety days.

Then they get a link they can paste into an email or an ATS note and forward to a colleague.

## 2. Why it is worth building

Every other page on the site argues your case at the reader. This one makes them describe their
own problem first, then tells them honestly whether you are the answer to it.

Three things follow from that:

- **It can say no.** A manager building an outbound SDR team scores 21 out of 100 and is told not
  to bother. That is the feature, not a bug. An instrument that flatters everyone persuades no one.
- **It is not gameable.** The questions are fixed in advance, the numbers are fixed in advance, and
  nothing the visitor clicks can raise your score above what your evidence supports.
- **It publishes its own distribution.** The page states, in plain text, that across all 50,000
  possible answer sets it scores between 20 and 96, median 70, and that 31 in 100 answer sets score
  below 65. No candidate publishes the odds their own assessment tool gives them. It is unfakeable,
  and it is only publishable because the model genuinely can say no.

It runs entirely in the browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, nothing is stored, there is no AI at
runtime. Their answers about their own business never leave their machine.

### What changed on 28 August, and why

The plan originally had **eight** dimensions, with a single one called "High-volume net-new
acquisition, cold outbound" capped at 0.25.

Brad challenged that, and was right. It was measuring the wrong thing. The cap came from the
cold-calling motion, but the evidence sitting underneath it is a record of **growing a business's
customer base**, which is a different capability and is evidenced at four separate employers, one
of them confirmed by the owner.

So dimension 8 was split in two. Growing a customer base now stands on its own at 0.85, backed by
99 Corporation, Hawke's Bay Tourism, Xchange and Canwest. Cold outbound as a daily motion keeps its
own low ceiling, 0.35, bounded by the honest fact that no dial or meeting volumes exist in the
record and that you left 99 Corporation partly because you did not enjoy it.

Demand generation rose 0.60 to 0.70 in the same pass, because the campaign, partnership and
packaging work at Adplus and Canwest was not being counted anywhere.

**This is now nine dimensions and the numbers behind the page have to be recomputed.** The
distribution quoted below was worked out on the old eight. It is here so you can see the shape of
the thing, and it will change.

## 4. Three examples of the scoring working

**These figures are from the old eight-dimension model and will change when the enumeration is
re-run on nine.** They are here to show the shape of the instrument, not as final numbers.

- **Retention-led manager** (churn at renewal, spend flat, pricing untouched for years, product
  bought and unused, measured on renewal rate, they are the channel partner): **95, Strong.**
- **Middle-of-the-road manager**: around **70**, "Substantial. Most of what you described, with
  real gaps named below."
- **SDR-led manager** (cannot get new logos, too young to have two-year accounts, volume not
  pricing, nobody has heard of them, measured on pipeline activity, direct sales): **21, Weak.
  "Not a good match."**

The report shows a band word plus a number rounded to the nearest 5, never a traffic light. 65 and
95 must not read as the same thing.

## 5. What the report shows them

1. **The band and the number**, with a verdict line in your voice.
2. **A bar chart.** One bar per capability they raised, plotting *how much they need it*, against
   a fixed reference line showing *how far your evidence reaches*. Where the bar overshoots the
   line, you are visibly not their person. Nothing they clicked can move the line.
3. **"Where I am not your person".** Only triggers on demand generation and cold outbound.
4. **"Standing limitations, shown on every result".** A separate heading, so a real finding does
   not get mistaken for boilerplate. Three permanent items: the save evidence is the education
   decade's only; accounts were lost on total cost against Google Classroom and Microsoft 365;
   MySimpleSiteMan, over $400,000 invested, closed, and the misjudgement named.
5. **"What I would need from you".** The flip. If they are a channel partner, you need the vendor
   relationship and a reporting line into it. If pricing has been static for years, you need the
   authority to move it or an agreed date when that conversation happens. Capped at three.
6. **Evidence cards**, each quoted sentence linking in one click to the actual letter scan.
7. **How this is scored**, including the published distribution.

Anything they did not raise is excluded entirely and shown as "You did not raise this". You are
never credited for a strength nobody asked about.

## 6. Things already decided, so you know they are not open

- Static scoring in the browser. No backend, no AI at runtime. Auditable before it ships.
- Ceilings fixed, never raised by answers.
- Gaps shown explicitly, under a heading separate from the standing limitations.
- The full score distribution published on the page.
- The share link is the main way to save it. `?print=1` shows the print layout on screen.
- `/fit/` is the one page allowed to say "you". Questions and the printed letter address the
  reader; your verdict lines, evidence and gaps stay first person. The exemption gets written into
  `PRODUCT.md` and `AGENTS.md` in the same commit.
- Not in version one: nav restructure, "I don't know" answers, per-question notes.

### The 99 Corporation reference

You said you already have the Nieuwland reference. **What form is it in?**

It matters because the site's own rule is that any claim quoted on a page must be verifiable in one
click against its source document. There are seven letter scans on the site already: Sancta Maria,
PowerSchool, Andrew Bergh, Rototuna, The Industry School, Canwest, Adplus and Hawke's Bay Tourism.
**There is no 99 Corporation one.** The register records Rob's numbers as a phone call on
14 August, not a document.

The 99 Corporation numbers are the strongest single piece of evidence behind the new dimension. If
the reference is a letter or an email that can be scanned, it goes in with the other seven and the
strongest card on the page gets a link like everything else. If it is verbal only, the card has to
say so and stand without one.
