Stage 3 — the recommendation

Direction A, Editorial — with one component taken from B

  • The brief rules out cold. Section 7 of the positioning brief says a direction that reads cold or corporate is off-brief even if it looks expensive. B is precise and it is also cold: it takes Adrian Pilgrim's sentence about warmth, patience and humour and puts it in a bordered panel headed Reference 01 of 03. That is the argument that ends B as a whole-site system, and it is not a small one.
  • The differentiator is documents. No competing personal site in this market publishes three signed, named references in full. That is the asset, and editorial typography is the native system for it — a serif at a wide measure over a warm ground is what a letter looks like. B turns a letter into a record and C turns it into a slogan.
  • The blog is a sales asset, not decoration. For the founder considering Clarity Engine AI, the writing is the pitch. A is the only one of the three that arrives as a reading system rather than a landing-page system, and /writing/[slug] is the highest-traffic route on the site by headcount.
  • C is the safest and the least distinctive. Very large grotesque over extreme whitespace is the most-copied premium look currently in circulation. It is not a template, but its failure mode is the exact one that has already caused two rejections — converging on a recognisable house style. It is the right answer if the priority ever shifts from being remembered to being unobjectionable.

The graft: B's evidence table into A's hero

  • Hero option G claims that three customers wrote references. B answers that claim with a three-row table of names, roles and links to the letters — the assertion and its receipts in one object. It is the single best component produced at this stage and it belongs in the chosen direction regardless of which one wins.
  • In A it would be set in Archivo at 13px on the existing hairlines, replacing the credit line currently under the support paragraph. Same information, more useful, and it gives the reader a second route into /proof from above the fold.
  • This is the same move Stage 1 made when it recommended hero option C with option A's closing sentence folded in. Nothing good is discarded because it arrived in the losing document.

What would change this recommendation

  • If the consulting buyer becomes the priority over the hiring manager. A's seniority turns from asset to liability the moment the primary reader is a founder assessing whether Brad is current on AI. In that case take C and graft A's serif into /writing only.
  • If Brad wants the site to read as a product rather than a publication. That is B, and it would be a defensible choice — but the voice would have to be rewritten alongside it, because the current copy is warm and the system is not.
  • If none of the three is right. Say so plainly and name what is wrong. A fourth direction is two hours of work at this stage and a fortnight of work after Stage 4.

What stages 1 and 2 decided

The position

  • Three-second proposition: Brad Friis keeps software customers, and grows them, for a decade at a time.
  • Hero line, chosen 10 August 2026: "Three customers wrote references I never asked for." Options A, C and E were turned down and a second round was written; the full set with the argument for and against each is in COPY/hero-lines/hero-lines.md. The Stage 1 recommendation, option C, is kept there as a rejected entry rather than deleted.
  • Consequence of the new hero: the claim names three documents, so three names now sit above the fold. Every direction carries the attributions in the same viewport, and written consent moves from important to structural.
  • Proof leads with the five-campus save, because it is the only asset that is a story, a commercial outcome and a signed third-party document at once. The 2,800 to 24,500 figure supports it rather than leading.
  • No claim appears without its evidence in the same viewport, and every quote links to the complete letter at /proof.

Changes to the approved plan

  • The audience lens is mostly rejected. The four resume variants share about three quarters of their content, so a visible control would re-order the same six achievements and ask the reader to classify themselves before they know anything. The URL parameter survives on /resume/[lens], set by Brad on outbound links, with no switcher shown.
  • "What I'm proud of" is cut from the homepage. No material exists for it. Community and governance move to /about.
  • /proof is both a destination page and distributed quotes, not one or the other.
  • The professional-services resume variant does not get a route — it duplicates the B2B SaaS variant. Kept as a document for direct sending.
  • Plan impact: the lens-switcher todo shrinks from cross-page state management to a single dynamic route on the resume.
