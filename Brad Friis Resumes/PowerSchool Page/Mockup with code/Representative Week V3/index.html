A week in the job.

I managed a ten-year channel partnership with one of the largest K-12 education technology providers in the United States. My role was to design and execute every strategy needed to sell, implement, support and maintain its solutions in New Zealand and Australian schools.

The week

The three pillars

Growth

Finding the right schools, winning them, and keeping them at renewal time.

  • Pipeline and renewals
  • Data and targeting
  • Campaigns and launches
  • Measurement and attribution

Delivery

Getting schools set up, trained and confident enough that the platform actually got used.

  • Technical implementation
  • Customer enablement
  • Learning design and facilitation
  • Support and service design

Operations

The money, the contracts, the vendor relationship and the team behind all of it.

  • Commercial and finance
  • Partnerships and suppliers
  • People and resourcing
  • Systems and governance

Beyond the platform partnership itself, no employer, customer, school or contract is named here, and no figures are given. The work is described at the level of what it required, which is the part that transfers.

That's the shape of the job.

One person held all of this, every week, for ten years. If some of it looks like work you need done, I'd enjoy the conversation.
