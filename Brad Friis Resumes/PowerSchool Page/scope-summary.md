# EdTech role — full scope summary

Status: working document, not site copy. Private — never publish this file or its
contents to the site as-is.

**Naming note:** the employer for this 10-year role is under a settlement gag and must
never be named on the site (see project memory `gct-never-named.md`). PowerSchool is
named here and may be named on-site — it was the vendor whose product (Schoology) the
employer resold, not the employer itself. Everywhere below, "the company" / "the
platform" refers to the employer; "PowerSchool" / "Schoology" refers to the vendor
relationship specifically.

This document is the raw capture of everything Brad did across the role, dictated in
one pass on 25 August 2026. It is organised into the categories he gave, in his order,
with his examples preserved. It is a source document for two things: (1) a page on
bradfriis.com making the case for his end-to-end edtech consulting/leadership
capability, and (2) a prompt for other LLMs to propose presentation formats, drafted
separately in this same folder.

## 1. Sales — strategy and delivery

- Prospecting
- Sales assets: presentations, demos, phone scripts, email automation sequences
- Lead generation
- Proposals
- EULAs (end user licence agreements)
- Financial projections and modelling

## 2. Implementation strategy — technical

- Technical implementation strategy and delivery

## 3. Customer onboarding

- Onboarding across every stakeholder group: leadership, staff, students, parents,
  support staff
- Bespoke onboarding paths per customer
- Training design and material development: video, self-paced courses, workshops
  (both generic and bespoke), webinars — design and delivery
- Generation of supporting marketing assets as needed, often one-pagers promoting new
  features or tools

## 4. Marketing

- Conferences: design and build of exhibition space, conference engagement strategy,
  lead generation strategy at conferences, presenting workshops at conferences
- Negotiated major sponsorship of several national conferences in exchange for
  software access to help run the conference
- Website design and development
- Marketing assets: email marketing, video
- Built and maintained a NZ-wide network of teachers and school leaders for sharing
  resources and ideas — approximately 650 teachers

## 5. Support

- Design and development of all support materials
- Response time standards
- MSA (master service agreement) framework

## 6. Financial strategy

- Billing design and development
- Accounts and payments
- SLAs (service level agreements)
- Invoicing design
- Annual renewal strategy and management of renewing customers
- Referral program design — built relationships and managed reciprocal referrals

## 7. Accredited MoE (Ministry of Education) facilitator

- Delivered MoE-funded training and strategic guidance to schools
- Technical support and design of an MoE integration tool — saved schools a minimum
  of 8 days of database work

## 8. Staff management

- Internal staff
- External contractors

## 9. Board reports

- Regular reporting to the board

## 10. International reseller relationship management

- Partnership contract negotiation and renewal
- Licensing and reseller terms
- Liaised across all stakeholder groups: sales, marketing, technical, support,
  renewals, leadership
