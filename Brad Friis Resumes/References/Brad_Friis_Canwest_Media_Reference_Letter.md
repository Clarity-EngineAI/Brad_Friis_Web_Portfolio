February 11th 2006

To Whom It May Concern,

Brad Friis was employed by CanWest Media Ltd. for a period of 2.5 years as a senior account manager. A solid client base was handed over with the position and Brad quickly learned how to successfully fulfill each of his client's individual needs.

Brad demonstrated exceptional abilities in all aspects of his role. His friendly and approachable manner made him a positive influence within his sales team and he was well liked. Brad quickly became known for thinking outside of the square and for creating new and untapped opportunities for his clients. Several of Brad's past clients have mentioned his ability a number of years on from his departure.

Monthly KPI targets were always achieved and Brad's dedication to his role resulted in consistent growth of his client base and overall billing for each quarter. He has a tenacious attitude toward his work and was often the leading member of his team. I found a highly commendable aspect of Brads approach to his role was the innate ability to discover new methods of attracting and securing new business in what can be a very difficult industry to work in. Brad has considerable ability in developing an idea to proposal and then to presentation to a client with a more often than not winning result.

Brad's resignation was of his own choosing and I would consider Brad a great asset to any business development and account management role. Without reservation I would like to recommend him for any position he might consider in what I expect will be a very bright and rewarding future.

Should you require any further information please don't hesitate to contact me on the details listed below.

Yours sincerely,

*[signature]*

Phillip Lemon

## What it evidences

- 2.5 years as a Senior Account Manager at CanWest Media Ltd, ending before February 2006.
- **"Monthly KPI targets were always achieved."** This is the signed, named, first-party version of
  the claim that [[Brad_Friis_Canwest_Sales_Figures]] is being considered as evidence for. It carries
  the same fact with none of the third-party exposure, which is the decisive argument for keeping the
  spreadsheet off the site.
- "Consistent growth of his client base and overall billing for each quarter" — growth of an
  inherited book, not a built one. The letter says the base was handed over. That is a retention and
  expansion claim, which is the same claim the GCT decade rests on, made twenty years earlier.
- A second employer stating the departure was voluntary. See
  [[Brad_Friis_Adplus_Advertising_Reference_Letter]].
- It fills a gap the CV leaves open. The master CV compresses 1999–2013 into two bullets and does not
  name CanWest at all.

## Cross-document notes

- **The letterhead is not the employer.** Lemon writes about CanWest Media Ltd on The Radio Network's
  paper and signs as The Radio Network. The ordinary reading is that he had moved to a competitor by
  the time he wrote it. A hostile reader could instead read it as a reference written on borrowed
  paper. The site should state Lemon's relationship in the caption — Brad's manager at CanWest,
  writing after both had left — rather than let the letterhead imply he was a Radio Network referee.
  This is a one-line fix that costs nothing and closes the question before it is asked.
- "Brads" appears without an apostrophe in paragraph three. In the original. Left as written.
- "fulfill" is the American spelling, in the original. Left as written.
