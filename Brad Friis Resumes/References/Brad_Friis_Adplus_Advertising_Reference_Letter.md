24 March 2003

**To Whom It May Concern**

Brad Friis has worked for Adplus for 15 months. During that time his duties included promoting the services of our company to prospective Advertising and Web based clients in a professional manner (his role with Adplus was obviously quite defined as are all roles within our organisation).

I say a 'professional manner' because finding people with Brad's skill is not an easy thing. He was always pleasant but somehow managed to obtain an extraordinarily high strike rate from which many clients were converted. Often he did so with little or no material backup what so ever. People with Brad's skill do not come along every day.

Brad was always very quick to learn and came to us with the attitude of wanting to grow and develop his skill base. He came highly recommended and achieved far [sic] during the period he was with us than I had imagined would be the case. Often too I found Brad putting in hours over and above that expected in order to improve his results.

Brad leaves us of his own accord and I take this opportunity to wish him all the very best for the future.

*[signature]*

AR Walker
Managing Director

## What it evidences

- Fifteen months at Adplus as at March 2003, written by the Managing Director.
- A conversion claim from the person who saw the pipeline: "an extraordinarily high strike rate",
  achieved "with little or no material backup what so ever". That last clause is the useful one —
  it is a claim about selling without collateral, which is a different and harder thing than selling.
- **"Brad leaves us of his own accord."** A departure stated as voluntary, by the employer, in
  writing, twenty-three years ago. [[Brad_Friis_Canwest_Media_Reference_Letter]] says the same thing
  independently about the next job. Two employers volunteering that the resignation was Brad's is a
  pattern, and it is the kind of thing a reader notices without being told.
