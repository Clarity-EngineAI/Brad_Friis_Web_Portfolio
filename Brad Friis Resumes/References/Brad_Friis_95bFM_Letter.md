# Formal Notice concerning Brad Friis — 95bFM

**Document:** Formal notice of service, addressed to Brad Friis personally, on 95bFM letterhead
**Date:** 9 February 2000
**Author:** Marianne Deady, Account Manager, 95bFM
**Brad's role at the time:** Organiser and primary contact for *2000ad The Event*, buying from 95bFM
**Relationship:** Vendor to client. Brad was the buyer, not an employee
**Signature:** Present, in black ink
**Source image:** `95 BFM.jpg`
**Consent:** Not sought as at 11 August 2026

**[95bFM letterhead — logo]**

Brad Friis
[home address withheld]

9th February, 2000

To whom it may concern.

This is a formal notice to state that Brad Friis was the primary contact for organising the advertising and promotion for **2000ad The Event** on our station, 95bFM.

Brad secured a 'presents' naming rights deal with our station, thereby negotiating that **2000ad The Event** received a discount of $18,490.00 over a period of 6 months.

Brad liased with both myself (and my predecessor Josh Hethrington) consistently over scheduling of ads, advertising copy, production of ads, station promotions and other associated tasks.

If you require any further information please do not hesitate to contact me on the numbers listed below.

Regards,

*[signature]*

Marianne Deady
Account Manager
95bFM

**On Josh Hethrington.** He is named neutrally, in passing, as a person who held a job. There is no
performance claim about him and nothing adverse. The exposure is small but it is not zero, and he has
not consented. Redacting the parenthesis costs the document nothing — the sentence reads cleanly
without it. **Recommend redacting to "(and my predecessor)".**

## What it evidences

- The earliest dated document in the archive, by a year. It moves the span from twenty-three years to
  **twenty-six**, and it moves the start of the record to 2000.
- One hard, checkable commercial number: **a negotiated $18,490.00 discount over six months**, secured
  by structuring the deal as station naming rights rather than as a media buy.
- Brad on the buy side. Every other document in the set has him selling. This one has him negotiating
  against a vendor, and winning on structure rather than on price. That is a different capability and
  the only evidence of it in the archive.

## Cross-document notes

- **This is not a character reference and should not be counted as one.** It makes no claim about what
  Brad is like to work with. It is a vendor confirming what a client did. Counting it in a "five
  people put it in writing" total would inflate the number with a document that does not do the job
  the number promises.
- The right home for it is `/record`, as the opening artefact of the career narrative, or as a
  secondary exhibit on `/letters` under a heading that distinguishes artefacts from references.
- "liased" is in the original. Left as written.
- *2000ad The Event* is set in bold in the original both times it appears.
- The letter is addressed to Brad at home, which means it was written for him to carry. That is the
  same publication basis as a "To Whom It May Concern" letter — see the consent argument in the
  session's publish/withhold table.
