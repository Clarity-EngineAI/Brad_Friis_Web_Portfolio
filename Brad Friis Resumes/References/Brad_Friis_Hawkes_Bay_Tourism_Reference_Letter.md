12 February, 2003

**TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN**

I have had pleasure in working with Brad Friis in his capacity as Business Development Manager of Adplus Ltd during the past two years. During this time a highly successful business partnership has formed between Hawke's Bay Tourism and this major regional advertising company.

It is my observation that Brad has assisted with a significant improvement in tourism industry buy-in to the core marketing activities of Hawke's Bay Tourism during this time. This transformation was the result of a deliberate strategy to improve the profile of Hawke's Bay Tourism with the local industry and to increase participation in our key marketing opportunities; namely the Hawke's Bay Visitor Guide and the regional website www.hawkesbaynz.com.

Brad ensured that service excellence was paramount and clearly recognised its vital importance in the highly competitive marketplace of today.

I am happy to recommend Brad to future employers. My observations are that he is an individual with integrity; plus the drive and dedication to reach and exceed sales targets.

Please do not hesitate to contact me if you require any further comment.

Yours sincerely

*[signature]*

Hamish Lowry
**General Manager**
[email withheld]

## What it evidences

- Brad held the title Business Development Manager at Adplus Ltd, and held it by February 2003.
- A named client-side General Manager attributes a measurable shift — industry buy-in to Hawke's Bay
  Tourism's core marketing — to a deliberate strategy Brad worked on.
- It names the two products behind the CV's "grew the Hawke's Bay Visitor Guide by over 30 percent a
  year": the Visitor Guide and the regional website.
- It is the earliest evidence in the set of Brad working the *account* rather than the transaction.
