26-02-2009

Deane Jessep
[home address withheld]

To any person considering Brad Friis for employment,

Over the last 7 years of knowing Brad Friis I have had the distinct pleasure of buying from him, working with him, utilising him as a consultant and best of all becoming his friend.

Historically we met when myself and many of the DJ's who worked for me at my nightclubs and events were drawn to purchasing numerous products from his music supply business; traits such as his impeccable taste, sense of style and willingness to step outside the norm regularly helped me keep my edge in what is an extremely difficult and competitive industry.

Being a driven self starter Brad and I naturally gelled and numerous times over the years I have found myself seeking his advice, particularly with problems stemming from within the very linked worlds of aesthetics and sales. In my view his grasp on what people want and how best to deliver it to them is simply unequaled. I and my business partner often brought Brad in as a design consultant to assist our ICT company with our clients in areas such as digital design, print media, and web development, an expert at delivering results Brad always had the solution or knew exactly where to go to get it.

Brads critical advice during my 2004 campaign for Napier City Council definitely contributed to my success. After that, his occasional wise council over a quiet drink would assist in moderating some of my more impetuous traits, consistently helping me to make better decisions for the city. Also of note was that more than once his problem solving skills and extremely good Hawke's Bay business network made me finding people to help with outside of the square community and business thinking much easier. His open policy of *"my connections are your connections"* is something that all people should take to heart when building any successful business network.

For all of the above and many other reasons I wish him the absolute best in any role he seeks; were I still running my own business rather than working for Vodafone I would employ him in an instant. Whichever company nabs Brad during what will be a very short time on the job market will be a very lucky company indeed.

Warm regards,

Deane Jessep
Vodafone Solutions Specialist
[mobile withheld]

**On the Vodafone sentence.** Jessep writes "were I still running my own business rather than working
for Vodafone I would employ him in an instant" — a hiring statement made in the same breath as his
employer's name. He is a Solutions Specialist, not a hiring manager, and the sentence is explicitly
conditional on a business he no longer runs. Reproduced without care it reads as Vodafone commenting
on a candidate. That is a live risk to a named third-party company, seventeen years on. If the letter
is published, the Vodafone reference should stay — cutting it would misrepresent the sentence — but
the caption must make clear that Jessep wrote in a personal capacity.

## The disclosure problem

The letter says **"and best of all becoming his friend"** in its first sentence, and describes advice
given "over a quiet drink". This is not a concealed conflict — Jessep discloses it himself, in the
opening line, which is to his credit and is the reason the document is usable at all. But it is a
friend's reference, and it must be presented as one.

The right handling is the opposite of the instinct. Do not bury the line or crop the sentence: lead
with it. A page that prints "best of all becoming his friend" in the pull quote and lets the reader
weigh it is a page that has earned the reader's trust on the other six. A page that quietly omits it
is one bad reader away from losing all of them. This is the same argument as
[[Brad_Friis_MySimpleSiteMan_Reference_Letter]] and it resolves the other way, for one reason: Jessep
disclosed and the surname did not.

**Recommendation: publish, with the relationship in the caption, and second-tier placement.** It goes
below the four professional letters, not among them.

## Cross-document notes

- **The filename is misleading.** `Napier City Councillor.jpg` names Jessep's office, not Brad's.
  Brad was not a councillor. The CV's community entry is Foodbank Hawke's Bay and Volunteering New
  Zealand. Anyone reading the archive cold will get this wrong. **Rename the source image
  `Deane Jessep.jpg`** at the same time the web derivatives are cut.
- **It is unsigned.** Typed name, no signature block, plain paper, no letterhead. Every other letter
  in the archive is signed. If the proof strip caption says "Written, signed, reproduced in full",
  this document does not satisfy it, and the caption must either change or the count must exclude it.
- Errors in the original, all left as written: "Brads" without an apostrophe; "wise council" for
  *counsel*; "unequaled" for *unequalled*; and the garbled clause "made me finding people to help with
  outside of the square community and business thinking much easier". Verified against the source
  image at full resolution on 11 August 2026. **The garbled sentence is a problem for publication**
  — a reader who stumbles on it stops reading. It is a fair argument for using a pull quote rather
  than the full text for this one document, and that is a departure from the page's own rule.
