## How scans are published, from 13 August 2026, 6:00 PM NZST

**Everything under `src/assets/letters/` is generated by `scripts/prepare-scans.py`. Never hand-edit
those files.** Rerunning overwrites them, and a redaction that exists only in a hand-edited output is
one nobody can audit. To change a redaction, change its coordinates in the script and rerun:

```bash
.venv/bin/python scripts/prepare-scans.py
```

All six published letters carry a scan. The script takes two kinds of source and gives each the
guarantee it needs.

The three oldest letters — Adplus, Hawke's Bay Tourism and CanWest — are built from the **4000×3000
originals in this folder**, not from `web/` and not from `redacted and cropped finals/`. Both of those
are downscales, and the versions in `redacted and cropped finals/` wrap images as small as 433×577,
which upscale to mush. The script paints the redactions, crops the cloth away by detecting the page,
and scales to an 1800px long edge. Painting happens before scaling, so the covered pixels are
destroyed rather than hidden.

The three newer documents are PDFs Brad redacted before export, so they are only rasterised, at the
DPI that lands on the same 1800px long edge. Two of them carry a **live text layer**, and a filled box
over live text is not a redaction — the text stays selectable. The script therefore refuses to publish
any PDF whose text layer still matches an email address or phone number. Both pass today because the
details were deleted rather than covered, which is the correct way to do it.

**Every redaction is the same light grey, `rgb(230, 230, 230)`**, matching the boxes Brad had already
baked into his PDF exports. Do not introduce white or black boxes: white pretends nothing was removed
and black reads as censorship.

### What is redacted, and what is not

Brad ruled on 13 August 2026 that **defunct organisational contact details may publish**. Hawke's Bay
Tourism left those premises over a decade ago, Adplus's numbers no longer reach anyone, and a role
address such as `adplus@adplus.co.nz` identifies no individual. Those letterheads now publish intact,
which also makes the documents more obviously genuine.

**A named individual's contact details always come off, however old.** Two are covered:
Hamish Lowry's work email below his signature on the Hawke's Bay letter, and Philip Lemon's email and
mobile below his on the CanWest letter. Lowry left Hawke's Bay Tourism more than twenty years ago and
it makes no difference; the address carries his name.

## Transcriptions are not all verbatim

**`Brad_Friis_The-Industry-School-Reference.md` is a structured digest, not a transcription.** It
carries invented section headings — Overview, Key Contributions, Strategic Alignment and Efficiency,
and others — and paraphrases the referee's sentences. None of that structure appears in the letter,
which is flowing prose.

Until 13 August 2026 the site published that digest as though it were Adrian Pilgrim's letter, and
drew its pull quote from the paraphrase. The quote read "Brad helped us see the platform's potential";
he actually wrote "Brad helped us see the platform in a new light", and "seriously considering" had
lost its adverb. Both are now corrected against the scan and `src/data/letters.ts` holds the letter as
written.

**Verify any transcription against its scan before quoting from it.** The other five were checked on
13 August 2026 and are faithful prose. A bolded label followed by a colon is the tell-tale sign of a
digest.

## Conventions

- **Verbatim means verbatim.** Original spelling, punctuation and errors are preserved and marked
  `[sic]` where they would otherwise read as transcription mistakes. The Rototuna smiley precedent
  stands: the letter is evidence, and evidence is not tidied.
- **Personal contact details are the one exception**, marked `[withheld]` inline. Any phone number,
  mobile, email or address belonging to a **named individual** comes off every document, however old
  it is and whether or not that person still holds the role.
- **Organisational contact details on a letterhead may stay**, by Brad's ruling of 13 August 2026,
  where the premises and numbers are defunct and the address is a role address rather than a person's.
  This applies to the scans; transcriptions keep marking them `[withheld]`, which costs nothing since
  the scan publishes alongside. If in any doubt about whether a detail is personal, withhold it.
- Each transcription carries a **personal data table** listing everything on the face of the document
  and how it is handled.
