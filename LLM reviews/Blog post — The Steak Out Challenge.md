**Eyebrow:** Field notes & stories

**Title:** The Steak Out Challenge

**Standfirst:** How a throwaway comment in a client meeting turned into a live broadcast, two
steaks, and one very unhappy sales manager.

---

Steak Out ran a challenge nobody had properly finished: two steaks, 359 grams each, eaten in one
sitting. The owner mentioned it partway through a client diagnostic meeting — the kind where you
sit with a business and work out what's actually going on before you propose anything.

My sales manager was in the room. He heard the number, decided it was nothing, and said so out
loud. Easy, he said.

I didn't say anything back. I just filed it.

The deal closed. The new advertising strategy went live. Life carried on as normal for about a
month, which is exactly long enough for a person to forget they ever said something like that in a
client meeting.

He was on leave when I organised the live broadcast.

He got three bites from finishing. Two glasses of red wine down, for what I assume he thought was
fortification. Visibly grey by the end of it. He stopped there.

The next morning, the office was still laughing about it when he came storming out of his own
office to find me.

"You little bast\*\*d, I haven't slept a wink all night, my guts been killing me, and I haven't been
able to go to the toilet since."

It pays to choose your words carefully.
