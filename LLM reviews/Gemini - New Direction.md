Case 1: The Lockdown Retention Operation (2020)
Meta: Enterprise Account Management · Pandemic Response · 0% Churn

Headline: 108 individual face-to-face sessions to hold an entire client base through global shutdown.

The Narrative:
When lockdown hit in 2020, standard communication channels broke down. Instead of sending mass updates or automated check-ins, Brad conducted 108 direct, one-on-one Zoom video sessions with key decision-makers across every client account.

The objective was double-edged: provide immediate operational support for remote transition while gathering direct intelligence on emerging client pain points.

The Result: Zero customer loss throughout the pandemic period. Relationships were deepened, leading directly to post-lockdown account expansions.

Ambient Validation:

"Empathetic and proactive... ensures that even the most hesitant team members become confident users." — Andrew Bergh, High School Principal

Case 2: Multi-Campus Turnaround & Expansion (2015–2025)
Meta: Multi-Campus Network · On-Site Intervention & Strategy

Headline: Rebuilding an enterprise relationship from active defect to multi-site expansion.

The Narrative:
A multi-campus organisation was actively evaluating alternative vendors to replace their core system platform. Rather than negotiating from a distance, Brad flew on-site for a multi-day diagnostic across every campus location—shadowing leadership, identifying friction points, and mapping user reality.

Using those insights, Brad built a tailored growth and engagement strategy. He then returned on-site to deliver phase two: executive alignment and hands-on staff enablement.

The Result: Retained the network mid-defection and subsequently expanded adoption across 5 campuses.

Ambient Validation:

"This clarity and customisation... gave us the confidence not only to stay with the platform, but to invest in getting the very best from it." — Adrian Pilgrim, Teaching & Learning Manager

Case 3: Structuring Hidden Revenue (2013)
Meta: 99 Corporation · Commercial Offer Structuring

Headline: Transforming unbilled operational support into a recurring revenue stream.

The Narrative:
Ongoing client support and technical assistance were being delivered reactively without dedicated commercial terms or line-item billing. Brad identified the gap between delivered value and billing structure, creating a formal managed-service offering billed annually in advance.

The Result: Expanded overall customer base by ~55% and raised income by ~30% during tenure, securing recurring revenue without increasing delivery overhead.
