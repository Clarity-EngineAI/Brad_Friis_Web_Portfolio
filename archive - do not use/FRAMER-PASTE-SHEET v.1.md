reference material. If a line isn't in this file, it isn't going on the site.

into Framer, tick the box. Three statuses only:

- `BLOCKED` — needs a fact only Brad has. Listed at the bottom so they don't stop the paste run.

Word limits come from the template's original copy. Do not exceed them — the layout breaks.

**Component now holds four fields per entry: title, subtitle, body, year.** Brad added the body

### The device — read this before pasting

The CV leads with the job title. This section leads with **what the job deposited**.

- **Title** = the lesson, written as a capability a buyer could hire.
- **Subtitle** = the actual role and context, so nothing floats.
- **Body** = the scene, and the one specific detail that makes the lesson true.

Why it works three ways: read only the titles down the page and you get a sales-and-delivery
playbook. Read the years beside them and you get thirty years of consistency, unbroken. Read the
bodies and you get the evidence. The chronology runs underneath for free, so the section isn't a
life story — it's an argument with dates attached.

**Body budget: 35 words hard ceiling.** Set by me, not the template — long enough to hold a scene
and a transfer, short enough that thirteen of them still scan. Check one against the live component
before pasting all thirteen.
