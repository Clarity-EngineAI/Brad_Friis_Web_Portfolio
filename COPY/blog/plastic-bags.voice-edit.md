## The draft

### The Territory

The school was raising money and the prize for selling the most was a scientific calculator.

I was eleven. I heard the word *calculator* and something in me went very quiet and very
focused.

They gave every student a sample pack — sandwich bags and freezer bags — and a form to
write the orders on. One sheet of paper. It had columns for the name and the quantity, and
you could see it had been made for selling to an aunty, and possibly a grandmother, and
then stopping.

The sheet was not going to be adequate.

So before I sold a single bag, I got a book.

---

We lived six kilometres out of Hastings, on a farm — ten acres of raspberries and thirty
thousand chickens. I had a Raleigh ten speed, light blue and silver. The samples went in my
school bag with the order book, and after school every day I rode into town and worked the
suburbs.

Akina. Frimley. I did not do the whole town. I did the parts that felt safe, and the way I
decided what was safe was the lawns. A well mown lawn meant a kind person lived there. That
was the entire system. I do not know where I got it from and I ran it the whole way through
without ever once questioning it, and I could not tell you now whether it was a good system
or whether I just never tested it.

Knock. Sample pack. Sandwich or freezer. Signature in the book.

Then move on, because there was no second sale to be had at that door. A person buys their
bags once.

Nobody was ever unkind to me. Not once. I have gone looking through that whole period for
the door that got slammed and there is not one in there. A no meant nothing at all to me. A
no was a house that was not the house, and the next one was thirty seconds away, and one of
them was going to be a yes.

Then the weekends, as well. It ran the whole of term three — two and a half months of it —
and by the second week it did not feel like a fundraiser any more, it felt like a job.

And at the end of every day, whenever the light started going and wherever my last house
had put me, I got back on the bike and rode the six or seven kilometres home. It depended
where I finished.

I do not think my parents ever fully understood the extent of it.

I think by that stage they had learned to just hold on and hope for the best.

---

There was an assembly at the end. The whole school in the hall, parents down the back, and
I went up in front of all of them and collected the calculator.

Afterwards the principal spoke to my mother, and she told me later what he had said, which
was that I had very nearly outsold the entire school combined.

I was not there for that part. It reached me secondhand, from my mother, in the kitchen,
some time after the fact.

I did not have a number of my own. Nobody ever gave me one. I had a book full of signatures
and a principal's sentence arriving via my mother, and that is the whole of the evidence.

---

The calculator was a Casio.

I want to be honest about the calculator. It was not the Casio I wanted. The Casio I wanted
was the one with Space Invaders built into it, which a boy could actually use.

I did not know what a cosine was. I had no reason to square anything.

It sat on my shelf.

---

## Change note

Twelve changes. All subtraction except two. Grouped by the test each failed. Every one is
reversible on its own — the original phrase is quoted in full.

### Test 3 — commentary (the largest group)

1. **"…the way it does before you decide a thing is already yours and you are simply
   waiting for everyone else to find out."** — Cut. This explains the quietness the
   previous clause has already shown, and it is the piece's most audibly composed sentence:
   two balanced clauses resolving on a knowing phrase. "Something in me went very quiet and
   very focused" is the whole beat. **This is the single biggest change in the pass.**
2. **"and standing about being liked was not what I was there for"** — Cut. Interpretation
   of the move-on, not a fact about it. "A person buys their bags once" is the reason, and
   it is already there. *Side effect: this also removes the sentence §4.3.3 flagged as the
   one a stricter reviewer would query for self-praise. It goes on voice grounds, not that
   one.*
3. **"That was the only part of the arrangement I would call work."** — Cut. Tells the
   reader how to weigh the ride home. The ride is described; the reader can weigh it. This
   is the closest thing in the piece to a stated moral, and cutting it strengthens the
   §3.1 position rather than weakening it.
4. **"and slightly flattened by the trip across"** — Cut. A written image, and the only
   metaphor in the piece. "It reached me secondhand, from my mother, in the kitchen, some
   time after the fact" is the fact of it. *Note: this phrase already survived one §4 flag
   as a repair to "journey". Removing it entirely resolves that flag rather than replacing
   it.*
5. **"the way most true things reach a child"** — Cut, with the above. It generalises from
   one memory to a category, which is commentary in the strict sense.

### Test 1 — symmetry

6. **"I looked at the sheet and I understood it was not going to be adequate."** →
   **"The sheet was not going to be adequate."** The original is a composed two-beat build
   with a formal verb in the middle. The judgement is what matters, not the looking.
7. **"the sheet was clearly built on the assumption that you would sell to an aunty"** →
   **"you could see it had been made for selling to an aunty"**. "Built on the assumption
   that" is written diction. Spoken is "you could see". *Keeps the joke exactly where it
   was and still never names the assumption.*
8. **"to this day that is the whole of the evidence"** → **"that is the whole of the
   evidence"**. "To this day" is a narrator flourish. The sentence lands harder without a
   phrase telling you it is still true.

### Test 2 — abstraction

9. **"for the whole campaign"** → **"the whole way through"**. "Campaign" is the writer
   reaching for the story's own theme; a boy on a bike does not call it a campaign. The
   word survives elsewhere in the record where it belongs.
10. **"I still could not tell you today"** → **"I could not tell you now"**. "Still" and
    "today" are doing the same job; speech uses one.

### Test 5 — read-aloud

11. **"A no was a house that was simply not the house"** → **"A no was a house that was not
    the house"**. "Simply" is a written intensifier. The flat version is more certain.
12. **"I think, by that stage, they had learned"** → **"I think by that stage they had
    learned"**. The commas are correct punctuation and wrong rhythm — they stage a pause a
    person telling this would not make.

### Considered and deliberately not changed

- **"something in me went very quiet and very focused"** — the doubled "very" is spoken
  rhythm, and elegant variation here would sound written. Kept, per *What to preserve*.
- **"Knock. Sample pack. Sandwich or freezer. Signature in the book."** — four fragments in
  a row, which is close to the banned cascade in §2.1. It survives because it is a
  procedure being recited, not emphasis being manufactured, and because it is the one place
  the piece imitates the shape of the work. Flagged here so the decision is on the record.
- **"It sat on my shelf."** — untouched. Test 4's model ending.
- **The Space Invaders paragraph** — untouched. It is the strongest thing in the piece and
  it is entirely remembered detail.
- **Word count** now approximately 590, down from 655. All of it commentary.

### Gaps — questions for Brad, not filled

1. **What the order book actually was.** An exercise book? A pad? Something from the farm?
   The interview says only "a book". It is the pivot of the whole story and it is the least
   visible object in it. One remembered detail here would be worth more than any sentence
   in this piece. **Not invented.**
2. **Where the calculator is now.** The ending is the shelf. If the object has a later
   location — a drawer, a move, thrown out, still owned — that is a stronger final fact
   than the shelf, in exactly the FORT COMMANDER shape. Only if true.
3. **What he said at the door.** The piece has the procedure but not the words. If there
   was a line he used every time, it belongs in the knocking paragraph.

### Changes that alter meaning rather than voice

**None.** Every cut removes interpretation the piece supplied about its own events. No
event, object, figure, place, name or sequence differs from the approved draft. §4 does not
need re-running on figures — all eight are present and unaltered, and the "two and a half
months / term three" sentence is untouched.

**One flag closes:** the §4 style flag on line 72 ("journey" → "the trip across") is
resolved by deletion. If Brad rejects change 4, the flag stands as previously recorded and
the repaired wording remains correct.
