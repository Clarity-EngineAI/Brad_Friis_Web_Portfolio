**Restatused 11 August 2026, twice in one day.** The first correction: the old caption was untrue —
Brad asked Bergh and Pilgrim for their references, so only Lad's was unsolicited.

**Second correction, after the archive audit.** See `design/01d-reference-archive-audit.md`.

- **The count is six, not five.** The seven new scans produced more references than the two expected,
  and two of those did not survive the audit — MySimpleSiteMan is withheld because the referee shares
  Brad's surname, and Jessep sits below the six as a disclosed friend rather than inside the count.
- **"Signed" has to go.** Jessep's letter is unsigned, and Pilgrim's and Lad's are not signed
  documents either. A caption that says signed while three of the set are not is the same class of
  error as the unsolicited claim, and it would be printed directly above its own refutation.
- ~~**"Four industries" earns its place.** Advertising, tourism, radio and education software. It is
  the fact that closes the hole the span argument exists to close — that all the evidence came from
  one friendly sector.~~

**Third correction, 11 August 2026, in phase 2. "Four industries" is dropped and does not go back.**
It is literally true and it does not survive checking: AR Walker was Brad's managing director at
Adplus and Hamish Lowry ran Hawke's Bay Tourism, which was an Adplus account. Advertising and tourism
are the same fifteen months, seen from opposite sides of the desk, and a reader who works that out
reads the number as padding. Full argument in `COPY/letters/letters-page.md` §2.

**"Two bosses and four customers" replaces it** and answers a sharper objection — customers like a
good salesman, so what did the people who managed him think? Both employers wrote one, and both
volunteered in writing that the resignation was Brad's own. Checkable against the documents on
`/letters`, and impossible to arrange.

Alternate, **available**, for a strip that wants no adjective at all: `6 references — 2003 to 2025`.
Quieter, and it makes the reader do the arithmetic, which is more convincing than being handed it.

**Unblocked 11 August 2026, fully.** Brad's decisions closed every dependency: the count is six, all
six have consented, and no publish/withhold call remains open. **One watch item** — Brad is requesting
a replacement reference from Deane Jessep. If it arrives, the figure and the caption both move, which
is why the count is nowhere in the hero display line.
