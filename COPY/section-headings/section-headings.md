## Hero

Alternates, both **available**: `Auckland · account management and customer success` — more useful

### Attribution line · **Restatused 11 August 2026 — three names became six**

> ~~Adrian Pilgrim, The Industry School · Dr Andrew Bergh, high school principal · Pranesh Lad,
> Rototuna High Schools~~

**Rejected 11 August 2026.** Not on taste. It names three of six, and it was written to prove a hero
line that is now dead. A hero support paragraph saying *six people* above an attribution line listing
three is the governing rule failing in the same viewport it is supposed to be enforcing.

**Replacement · Recommended, paired with hero option J**

> Andrew Bergh · Pranesh Lad · Adrian Pilgrim · Phillip Lemon · AR Walker · Hamish Lowry

Six names, no roles — the roles are on `/letters`, one click away, and six name-and-role pairs is a
paragraph rather than a line. Ordered newest document first, matching the order of the letters page,
so a reader who scans the line and then clicks finds them in the same sequence.

**Against.** Six surnames with no context is close to meaningless to a cold reader, and it is 78
characters, which is long for small type under a support paragraph. It works only because the
sentence directly above it has just said what the six people did.

**Mobile alternate · Available**

> Six names, six organisations, 2003 to 2025 — in full at Who says so

Loses the names, which is the whole point of the line, and keeps the proof that there are six of
them. Use only where the full list wraps to three lines.

**Coupled to the hero.** If Brad picks hero option K instead of J, the display line itself carries
the claim and this line still has to appear — the rule does not change with the option.

### Calls to action · **Restatused 11 August 2026**

> Read all six — primary
> Take the CV — PDF — secondary

**Primary, changed from `Read the references`.** Still available and still correct, but *Read all
six* names what the reader gets, follows directly from the last sentence of the hero support
paragraph, and carries the count where the count is cheap to change. `Read the letters` is
**rejected**: two of the six are not letters.

**Secondary, changed from `Download the resume (PDF)`.** The CV decision settled on 11 August 2026 —
route `/cv`, file `Brad-Friis-CV.pdf`, button `Take the CV — PDF`. New Zealand and Australian hiring
managers say CV. See `COPY/navigation/navigation.md`.

Rejected alternates, unchanged: `View my work` — says nothing. `Get in touch` — wrong ask at forty
seconds; the hiring manager wants evidence, not a conversation, and the contact route is in the
header anyway.

## Proof strip

Four figures, stated once, no elaboration. **In use** from Stage 2.

| Figure | Caption |
| --- | --- |
| 2,800 → 24,500 | Licensed users, grown over a decade |
| 10 years | One vendor relationship, held exclusively |
| 5 campuses | Retained mid-competitor-evaluation, then expanded |
| ~~3 references~~ ~~5 references~~ **6 references** | ~~Written, signed, unsolicited~~ ~~Written, signed, reproduced in full~~ ~~Six people, four industries, reproduced in full~~ **Two bosses and four customers, 2003 to 2025** |

**Restatused 11 August 2026, twice in one day.** The first correction: the old caption was untrue —
Brad asked Bergh and Pilgrim for their references, so only Lad's was unsolicited.

**Second correction, after the archive audit.** See `design/01d-reference-archive-audit.md`.

- **The count is six, not five.** The seven new scans produced more references than the two expected,
  and two of those did not survive the audit — MySimpleSiteMan is withheld because the referee shares
  Brad's surname, and Jessep sits below the six as a disclosed friend rather than inside the count.
- **"Signed" has to go.** Jessep's letter is unsigned, and Pilgrim's and Lad's are not signed
  documents either. A caption that says signed while three of the set are not is the same class of
  error as the unsolicited claim, and it would be printed directly above its own refutation.
- ~~**"Four industries" earns its place.** Advertising, tourism, radio and education software. It is
  the fact that closes the hole the span argument exists to close — that all the evidence came from
  one friendly sector.~~

**Third correction, 11 August 2026, in phase 2. "Four industries" is dropped and does not go back.**
It is literally true and it does not survive checking: AR Walker was Brad's managing director at
Adplus and Hamish Lowry ran Hawke's Bay Tourism, which was an Adplus account. Advertising and tourism
are the same fifteen months, seen from opposite sides of the desk, and a reader who works that out
reads the number as padding. Full argument in `COPY/letters/letters-page.md` §2.

**"Two bosses and four customers" replaces it** and answers a sharper objection — customers like a
good salesman, so what did the people who managed him think? Both employers wrote one, and both
volunteered in writing that the resignation was Brad's own. Checkable against the documents on
`/letters`, and impossible to arrange.

Alternate, **available**, for a strip that wants no adjective at all: `6 references — 2003 to 2025`.
Quieter, and it makes the reader do the arithmetic, which is more convincing than being handed it.

**Unblocked 11 August 2026, fully.** Brad's decisions closed every dependency: the count is six, all
six have consented, and no publish/withhold call remains open. **One watch item** — Brad is requesting
a replacement reference from Deane Jessep. If it arrives, the figure and the caption both move, which
is why the count is nowhere in the hero display line.
