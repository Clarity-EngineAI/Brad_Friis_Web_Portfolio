## Answer 4 — the two-year redesign cycle, and the $70k account

**Given 14 August 2026, verbatim:**

> rembrandt Fine Arts an art and picture framing wholesaler invested in a 70K website that had full

### The two-year cycle is the most underrated line in this file

**"Every 2 years I would resell a redesign of websites to clients, and a refresh of the copy."**

**That is a renewal motion in an industry that does not have renewals.** A web build is a one-off
project; the client owns the site and can walk away permanently the day it ships. **Brad turned a
project business into a repeating one by going back on a cycle.**

**It also explains the copy service line from Answer 3.** He brought in copywriters, then resold copy
refreshes as part of the two-year cycle. **The two answers lock together: he built the service line
that made the recurring sale possible.** That is a strategy, not two unrelated moves.

**The bar.** "Every 2 years" is how he describes his practice, **not a measured renewal rate**. It
must never become "a 100 per cent renewal rate", "all clients renewed every two years", or any
count. **The claim is that he worked a repeat cycle, not that every client took it.**
