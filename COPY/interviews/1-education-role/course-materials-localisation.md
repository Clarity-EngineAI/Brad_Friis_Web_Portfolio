# Course materials — localised from the US and sold

**Source:** Brad, spoken, 13 August 2026, volunteered unprompted as "another chargeable item I
created".

**The fifth revenue line recorded this session.** It also **answers open question 7 in
`area-2-onboarding-adoption.md`** — whether the course library was Brad's design or the vendor's. The
answer is neither, exactly, and the real answer is more interesting.

---

## Key points

**Brad purchased course materials from the United States, reworked them for New Zealand and Australia,
and sold them to schools alongside on-site training.**

**The localisation was thorough, and he listed the work himself:**

| What was changed | Why it signals more than editing |
| --- | --- |
| **All the photos replaced** | A US classroom photograph is instantly wrong to a NZ teacher. This is the detail that shows he understood the audience would reject the source material on sight |
| **Reworded throughout** | Vocabulary, spelling and register differ across the two education systems |
| **Aligned to New Zealand and Australian education** | Curriculum and pedagogical alignment — the substantive work |

**Sold as a package with on-site training**, both chargeable.

### This is the second instance of a method already recorded, and the two corroborate

`design/01-positioning-brief.md` §11.1 correction 3 records that Brad was **trained on the vendor's
enablement programme as the local partner, then rebuilt it for New Zealand and Australia** — described
there as *"a programme written for another market, reworked for this one"*. That instance was noted as
having **no contents**: the claim existed with nothing under it.

**This answer is either those contents, or a second independent instance of the identical method.**
Either outcome is valuable:

- **If it is the same work**, the enablement claim finally has substance — photos, wording, curriculum
  alignment, and a price.
- **If it is separate**, Brad localised foreign material for this market **twice**, which makes it a
  method rather than an occasion.

**Ask which. Do not merge them on the assumption that they are the same thing** — see open question 1.
`_open-questions.md` §5 item 2 already flagged this connection as one to test; **it is now testable.**

### Why the photos are the strongest detail

**Brad listed "changed all the photos" first, ahead of the wording and the curriculum alignment.** It
reads as a minor production note and it is the most revealing thing in the answer.

Teachers rejecting material that is visibly not about them is the same problem as the chalkboard
teachers in `change-management-and-the-divide.md` — **adoption fails on credibility before it fails on
content.** Someone who buys a course library and ships it unchanged is reselling. Someone who replaces
every photograph understands that a teacher in Hamilton will not accept a US high school as a picture
of their own classroom.

**It is the same instinct as the four already tabled in `area-1-five-campus-save.md`:** meet the
customer in their own context rather than presenting the product in yours.

## The detail

> "Another chargeable item I created was on-site training and access to course materials that I built
> and purchased from the United States and then reworked — changed all the photos, reworded, and
> aligned New Zealand and Australian education, and then made them available as a sale to schools."

### "Built and purchased" — an ambiguity that must be resolved before publication

**The phrase is self-contradictory as spoken.** *Built* and *purchased* are different acquisitions, and
the claim changes materially depending on which is meant:

| Reading | The claim | Strength |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purchased, then reworked | **Licensed foreign material and localised it for this market** | Strong, honest, and matches the rest of the sentence |
| Built some, purchased others | A mixed library | Needs the split before either half is claimable |
| Built it himself | Original authorship | **Not supported by the rest of the sentence**, which describes modifying existing material |

**The safe published form is the localisation**, which is what the detail of the answer actually
describes. **Do not publish "built" without resolving this** — claiming authorship of purchased
material is the kind of error that collapses on a single question at interview.

**Note also that `area-2-onboarding-adoption.md` records Brad building "countless courses" across the
platform's functional strands at three levels each.** Whether that library and these purchased
materials are the same thing, or two separate bodies of work, is now an open question that touches both
files.
