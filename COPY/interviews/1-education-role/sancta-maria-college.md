## The detail

### The origin — and why it validates the conference spend

> "I met the principal of Sancta Maria High School at a conference that I was speaking at."

**This closes a loop opened earlier in the session.** `conferences-and-sponsorship.md` records the
speaking and the NASDAP sponsorship, and its open question 3 asks whether any account can be traced
back to a conference. **One can, and it is this one.**

That converts the conference activity from a branding claim into a demand-generation claim with a
named customer attached. **A speaking slot that produced a deal is a different order of evidence from
a speaking slot that produced exposure.**

### The competitive context, which Brad stated almost in passing

> "[They] travel[led] to the United States to investigate learning management system software
> solutions."

**A school that flies to the United States to evaluate LMS platforms is running a serious, funded,
international selection process.** It was not a school waiting to be sold to. It had already gone
looking, at its own expense, on the other side of the world.

**Winning that is a competitive win against a considered field**, and the site currently has no
competitive-win story at all. Note the direction of travel too: they went to the US to look, and
bought from the New Zealand channel partner.

**This is also the counterweight to the hunter-or-farmer objection from the opposite side.** Most of
this session's evidence is retention. **This is origination** — a new logo, won against an international
evaluation, from a cold conference meeting. `design/01-positioning-brief.md` §3.1 records that the site
proves *keeps* four ways and *grows* barely at all. This proves *wins*, which the site is also short of.

### The outcome

> "Within one term, [they] had onboarded one hundred per cent of their teachers to using it daily in
> their classroom."

**Three things make this the strongest single figure of the session:**

1. **100 per cent is a ceiling, not an estimate.** It cannot be rounded up and it cannot be disputed
   as vague.
2. **"Using it daily in their classroom" is a usage standard, not a login count.** Most vendors report
   provisioned accounts. This is a claim about actual practice.
3. **One term is a school term — roughly ten weeks.** It is a bounded, checkable period every reader
   understands.

**Set against the rest of the role, it is the proof that the method scales down as well as up.** The
five-campus save took two trips to Queensland. Sancta Maria took one term to reach every teacher.

### The renewal — eight years, and what it settles

> "Sancta Maria have been renewing their contract for the past eight years."

**This is the answer to blocker 1's outstanding requirement and it arrived without being asked for.**

Blocker 1 established that more than one long-held account grew, which licensed the plural in hero
candidate P. `0-blockers.md` recorded it as *partially banked* — the plural confirmed, the instances
not. **Sancta Maria is a named account, held eight years, with a documented start and a countable
renewal history.**

**Why it is the strongest of the named accounts, ahead of Rototuna, Elim and Mackenzie:**

| Account | Won | Adopted | Held | Grew |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Sancta Maria** | **Yes — competitively, from a US search** | **Yes — 100% in one term** | **Yes — 8 years** | Not yet established |
| Rototuna | not stated | not stated | not stated | Yes — 600 → 1,200 → ~2,300 |
| Elim | not stated | not stated | not stated | Yes — second and third campus |
| Mackenzie | not stated | not stated | not stated | Yes — Australia and the US |
| The Industry School | not stated | Yes — the week and the strategy | Yes — retained under threat | Yes — "expanded investment", unquantified |

**Eight renewals is a harder retention claim than "held for ten years".** A decade-long relationship
can be inertia. Eight *renewal decisions*, each one a moment when the school could have left and did
not, is eight separate verdicts by the customer.

**And it dates the account.** Eight years of renewals from a September 2025 departure places the win at
roughly 2016–2017 — early in the role, and consistent with the promotion sequence. **This is the first
datable event in the entire education decade.** Confirm with Brad rather than publishing the inferred
year.

**One caution.** Brad says "have been renewing", present perfect, and he left the role in September
2025. Whether the eight years run to his departure or to now needs establishing — it changes whether
the account is a live relationship or a historical one, and the site must not imply he still holds it.

### The comparison claim — flagged

> "No school has ever been remotely close to that quick to transform the way that teaching and learning
> occurred in their organisation."

**This is unpublishable as stated, and the reason is not the register.** Unlike the five
self-assessments recorded elsewhere today, this is not self-praise — it is a **factual claim about
every other school**, and Brad cannot evidence it. "No school has ever" is an absolute over a
population he did not measure.

**The publishable form is the bounded one:** the fastest adoption he saw across the decade, or the
fastest of the schools he onboarded. That is a claim about his own portfolio, which he can stand
behind, and it loses almost nothing.

**Precedent:** hard rule 2 forbids promoting "one of" to "the" in reference quotes. The same discipline
applies to Brad's own claims. **"The fastest I saw" is defensible. "No school has ever" is not.**

### What to ask him for, and what not to

**Brad's offer covers three things: the work, his character, and the achievement.** Character is the
one to de-emphasise, and this needs saying plainly because it cuts against instinct.

The archive is already rich in character evidence — Bergh on supporting resistant teachers, the
Rototuna note, Lemon on clients remembering him years later. **What the archive has none of is a
customer confirming a commercial outcome.** The highest-value version of this letter is the one that
says what happened: that the school ran an international search, chose the platform, had every teacher
using it inside a term, and has renewed since.

**A warm letter about a good bloke adds a seventh voice to a chorus that already has six. A letter
confirming the adoption figure and the renewal history is the only document of its kind in the
archive.**

**Do not draft it for him.** Hard rule 2 and the entire evidential posture of this site depend on
letters being the referee's own words. The correct approach is to tell Nyke what the site is for and
what Brad is applying for, and let him write it.
