## The role is closed — 13 August 2026

**All seven capability areas are covered.** Area 6 was the last, and it closed in three answers.

**The commercial-mechanics half is no longer the weak half.** When these interviews began, areas 3, 4,
5 and 6 — pricing, contracts, billing, support structure — were the half an account management employer
reads for, and the half with nothing under it. The argument now runs end to end:

- **A designed six-line revenue model** for the sector, with the mandate behind it (A34, A35)
- **A renegotiated vendor contract** with three named terms, one of which made every retention claim in
  the archive structurally possible (A37–A40)
- **A support product created from an activity being given away free**, priced, banded by school size,
  billed a year in advance, **and specified down to the hours and the response obligation** (A21–A23,
  A32, A33, A43–A46)
- **A save that expanded into four revenue events** (A16–A18)
- **A created revenue line** in the SIS integration (A28–A30) and **a government accreditation** (A25)

**One caveat carries into Phase 3 and it is the seam most likely to be pushed at interview.** Areas 3
and 6 are one product. **Never publish them as two.**
