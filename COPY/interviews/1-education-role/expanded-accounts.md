## Key points

**Five distinct expansion instances named, plus one territory award.** Blocker 1 asked for more than
one. Brad gave five, in four different shapes — geographic expansion, campus expansion, roll growth,
and retention-under-budget-pressure that held rather than grew. That range matters more than the
count: it is the difference between an anecdote and a pattern.

**The strongest single instance for the site is Rototuna**, because it is the only one with a
before-and-after number attached, and the number is large.

**The Australian territory award is not an account story and should not be filed as one.** It is the
land-and-expand argument told about Brad rather than about a customer, and it is the cause of the
second promotion. Cross-filed to area 7.

## The COVID interview programme — cross-filed to capability area 2

**108 interviews with teachers**, conducted through COVID-19, to support them to use the tools more
effectively and to support their students in lockdown. Brad: "this was hugely successful."

**This is the single most concrete piece of adoption evidence produced so far, and it was not in the
brief.** Capability area 2 — the onboarding, training and adoption programme — was documented in the
master resume as "hands-on and remote workshops for non-technical staff". That is a description of a
service. *One hundred and eight interviews* is a countable act, and the count is the evidence.

**Why it is strong:** it is a number nobody would invent, it is specific to a period every reader
lived through, and it is one-to-one work at a scale that implies method rather than heroics. It also
sits directly beneath the Bergh letter's existing quote about supporting teachers resistant to change
— that letter now has an activity underneath it, not just a sentiment.

**"Hugely successful" is Brad's assessment and is not publishable as stated** — it is the
unfalsifiable self-assessment class banned at `design/01-positioning-brief.md` §7. The 108 is
publishable; the adjective is not. **The count does the praising.**

## The Australian territory award — cross-filed to capability area 7 and the promotion sequence

**"We also earned the Australian market through PowerSchool, which was given to us as a result of high
performance and excellent systems and our deep understanding of the education sector and commitment to
the products that we were selling."**

This corroborates LinkedIn line 52 — *"After continued success in the New Zealand market, GCT Education
was awarded management of the Australian market"* — and adds the vendor's stated reasons.

**Four reasons the territory was awarded, in Brad's words:** high performance; excellent systems; deep
understanding of the education sector; commitment to the products.

**Why this is a different class of evidence from everything above.** Every other item on this page is
Brad growing a customer. This is Brad's *employer* being grown by the vendor because of how the
territory was run — a principal choosing to hand over more market. It is the land-and-expand argument
told about Brad himself, and it is currently nowhere on the site.

**Note the pronoun.** Brad says "we" and "us" throughout. He is describing a company award, not a
personal one. Any site copy must not convert the company's award into a personal one — that is the
same class of overstatement as promoting "one of" to "the" under hard rule 2. The honest form is that
he ran the territory whose performance won the expansion.
