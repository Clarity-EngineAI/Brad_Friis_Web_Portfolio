## 2 — The questions that would turn Tier B into Tier A

**The single most valuable use of the next interview session.** Each converts a real claim from
unpublishable to publishable.

1. **What did the five-campus account buy after they stayed?** More campuses, licences, product, a
   longer term, managed services — and roughly what it did to the account's value. **This is the half
   the target role pays on, and the site already claims it (`bradfriisresumemaster20260806.md` line
   21) without evidencing it.**
2. **Two named schools that considered leaving on budget and were held.** Who, roughly when, how Brad
   found out, what he did, and whether the spend held flat or rose. B2 is a plural claim with no
   instances, which is the exact failure that killed two hero candidates.
3. **The think tanks: how many sessions, how many principals, and what came out of them?** B3
   publishes with specifics or not at all. **Was Andrew Bergh one of them?**
4. **Mackenzie and Elim in numbers.** Any magnitude at all.
5. **Of the 108 interviews, roughly how many teachers started negative?** Asked three times, not yet
   answered. Turns A14 from warm to measured.
6. **One named instance of the leadership/faculty divide** — which school, what the disagreement was,
   what Brad did, what happened after. A12 is currently a general claim.
7. **A Google school and an Apple school: what actually differed between the two onboardings?** A11's
   concrete instance.
8. ~~**What proportion of the book took the managed services agreement?**~~ **ANSWERED 13 August 2026:
   90 per cent of clients were on a support agreement.** The prediction held — this is the better
   claim, and the $40–50k drops to supporting detail. Recorded at `1-education-role/area-3-managed-services.md`.
   **Companion answer, same session: all NZ schools were on the SIS integration**, closing the third
   unasked attach-rate question at `1-education-role/sis-integration-product.md`. **Neither figure is
   ever multiplied by a price band.**
9. **What was the managed services contract term?** The last unanswered part of area 3's original
   question. Monthly structure billed annually in advance (A22) is the billing shape, not the term.
